## 2. Tracking check: real drop or broken tracking?

Rule: alarm when the weekly AI-referral share falls below half of the median of the 8 weeks before.

```text
  week 2025-06-02: AI share 0.7% (expected about 2.9%), AI sessions 134, direct 4,606
  week 2025-06-09: AI share 0.6% (expected about 2.9%), AI sessions 123, direct 4,696
  week 2025-06-16: AI share 0.7% (expected about 2.9%), AI sessions 137, direct 4,615
```

- The rule finds exactly the planned incident weeks (June 2025).
- Signs of a tracking problem, not a demand problem: the drop is sudden, it starts on a release date, and it ends just as suddenly. Real demand changes are gradual.
- Action: annotate the weeks in every report and do not use them for channel decisions.

## 5. Before/after: did automotive get worse in H2 2025?

Deals created before vs from 1 Jul 2025, decided deals only.

```text
  automotive     before 19.2% (n=177)  after 4.5% (n=44)  diff -14.7 points, 95% CI -21.9 to -2.9, p = 0.006
  it_software    before 28.1% (n=235)  after 30.0% (n=60)  diff +1.9 points, 95% CI -9.9 to +15.5, p = 0.717
  pharma         before 32.9% (n=167)  after 23.4% (n=47)  diff -9.5 points, 95% CI -21.8 to +5.8, p = 0.225
```

Difference-in-differences (comparison group: all other industries):

```text
  change automotive     -14.7 points
  change other industries +0.3 points
  difference            -14.9 points
  logistic regression interaction automotive x after: odds ratio 0.20, p = 0.035
```

- Confirmed: automotive fell more than the other industries in the same period (p = 0.035). Because the comparison group had the same season and market, the drop is specific to automotive.
- Still a small base (44 decided automotive deals after 1 Jul 2025): the size of the drop is uncertain, the direction is not. The loss reasons (section 4) point to price.
